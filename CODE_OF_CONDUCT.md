# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a welcoming, harassment-free space for everyone, whatever their age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and respectful, and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what's best for the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery.
- Publishing other people's private information without permission.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to **fazalabbas2002@gmail.com**. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, commits, or contributions that break this code, and may ban contributors who repeatedly do so.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
